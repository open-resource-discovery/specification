# Governance

The Open Resource Discovery (ORD) project is governed by the **ORD Steering Committee** under the [NeoNephos Foundation](https://neonephos.org/projects/open-resource-discovery-ord/), a [Linux Foundation Europe](https://linuxfoundation.eu) project.

The authoritative governance materials — committee composition, decision-making process, charter, meeting cadence, and contribution policies — are maintained in the dedicated steering repository:

> **[github.com/open-resource-discovery/steering](https://github.com/open-resource-discovery/steering)**

This document summarizes how that governance interacts with the Community Specification License process for the specification developed in this repository.

## 1. Roles

- **Steering Committee** — Strategic direction, scope, and approval of new specification versions. See the [steering repository](https://github.com/open-resource-discovery/steering) for current membership.
- **Maintainers** — Day-to-day technical stewardship of the specification, review of pull requests, consensus determinations, and coordination of appeals.
- **Editor** — Ensures that the specification accurately reflects approved decisions and follows the project's formatting and content rules.
- **Contributors** — Anyone who submits a Contribution under the project's accepted contribution terms.
- **Participants** — All Contributors collectively, subject to the Community Specification License.

The Working Group must formally designate one or more Maintainers and an Editor. Their appointments and the canonical roster remain open governance items listed below.

## 2. Decision Making

**2.1. Consensus-based.** Decisions on the specification are made by consensus among Maintainers, informed by input from Contributors and the Steering Committee. The agreement of all Participants is preferred but not required; Maintainers determine consensus in good faith based on the dominant view and the substance of objections. Evidence of consensus must be recorded in a public pull request, issue, or published meeting minutes.

**2.2. Appeals.** A decision may be appealed via a GitHub issue or pull request. Appeals are considered by the Maintainers in good faith and receive a written response within a reasonable time. Unresolved appeals may be escalated to the Steering Committee.

## 3. Ways of Working

The project follows the consensus-based due process principles described in [Section 3 of the Community Specification Governance Policy 1.0](https://github.com/CommunitySpecification/Community_Specification/blob/8a05e46d768ac0525af882b619c42f3380a9e869/05-governance.md): openness, lack of dominance, balance, coordination and harmonization, consideration of views and objections, and written procedures.

Information disclosed in connection with public Working Group activities, including meetings, Contributions, and submissions, is not confidential regardless of any marking to the contrary. If the Working Group uses a private repository, its contents must not be publicly disclosed without Working Group Approval.

## 4. Specification Development Process

ORD follows the four stages defined by the Community Specification process: **Pre-Draft**, **Draft Specification**, **Working Group Approval**, and **Publication**. Technical releases are cut from `release/vX.Y.Z` branches, tagged, and recorded in [`CHANGELOG.md`](./CHANGELOG.md). The procedure for granting Working Group Approval and publishing an Approved Specification remains an open governance item.

No Draft Specification or Approved Specification may be submitted to an external standards development organization (e.g., ISO/IEC or OASIS) without Working Group Approval. Any additional approval required by the Steering Committee remains applicable.

## 5. Code of Conduct

All participants are subject to the [LF Europe Code of Conduct](https://linuxfoundation.eu/policies/code-of-conduct). See [`Notices.md`](./Notices.md) for the designated points of contact.

## 6. Open Governance Items

The following items remain unresolved and should be completed and recorded as part of ORD's Community Specification adoption:

- Appoint one or more Maintainers and an Editor, and publish the canonical roster.
- Decide how contributors record assent to the Community Specification contribution terms.
- Define the exact approval, review, exclusion, publication, and immutable-release procedure for Draft and Approved Specifications.
- Identify the repository or document containing the binding governance rules if the steering repository remains authoritative.
- Appoint two project-specific confidential Code of Conduct reporting contacts in [`Notices.md`](./Notices.md).

## 7. Amendments

This governance document may be amended by approval of the Steering Committee, recorded in the [steering repository](https://github.com/open-resource-discovery/steering).
